Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Nepali Utilities ("this site", "we") provides free tools and daily widgets. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site and how it's handled.

What we don't collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address or any other personal details. The tools on this site, such as the number to Nepali words converter, run entirely in your browser — the numbers you type are never sent to us.

Data stored in your browser

Two small preferences are saved using your browser's local storage, so they're remembered the next time you visit:

  • Your choice of 12-hour or 24-hour time format.
  • The city you last selected in the weather widget.

This information stays on your device. We don't have access to it, and it isn't sent to our servers because this site doesn't have a server-side database — it's a static set of files.

Weather data

The weather widget sends the city name you select to WeatherAPI.com to fetch current conditions. See WeatherAPI.com's privacy policy for how they handle that request.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more and opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and read more generally at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising partners may also use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to collect information for measurement and ad personalization. This site does not control those technologies directly.

Analytics

If we add analytics in future (for example, to see which tools are used most), this policy will be updated to name the service used and what it collects.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the details on our About page.
