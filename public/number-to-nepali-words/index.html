Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a number to Nepali words?

Type any number in English or Nepali digits. The tool writes it in Nepali words using लाख, करोड, अर्ब and खर्ब where needed.

Can I use Nepali digits like १२३?

Yes. You can enter English digits (123) or Nepali digits (१२३). Both are supported.

Is this useful for cheques and documents?

Yes. Writing amounts in words is common for cheques, legal papers and official forms in Nepal. Always double-check important documents.
