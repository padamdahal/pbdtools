Frequently asked questions

How do I type Nepali using an English keyboard?

Type phonetically in Roman letters. For example, namaste becomes नमस्ते. Double vowels for long sounds: aa → आ, ee → ई, oo → ऊ.

Is this Nepali Unicode or Preeti font?

Output is standard Unicode Devanagari. Paste into Facebook, WhatsApp, Gmail, Word, and government forms without installing Preeti.

Does my text leave the browser?

No. Conversion runs on your device. Drafts stay in local storage only.

How do I type half letters (halant)?

Type a consonant without a following vowel. Example: k → क् and ka → क.
