    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Rasuwa flood map?

    An interactive browser map of the 26 August 2026 Bhotekoshi–Trishuli flash flood in Rasuwa and downstream districts. It loads open Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT) damage layers from the Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX).

    Where does the damage data come from?

    Open GeoJSON published on HDX by HOT: observed flood extent (27 August 2026), AI and manually validated building damage, destroyed/damaged OSM features, bridge damage assessment, and exposed hydropower sites.

    Can I hide or show individual layers?

    Yes. Use the checkboxes in the left panel. Heavier layers (building damage) load only when you turn them on.

    Is the news panel live?

    It requests recent public headlines about the Rasuwa / Bhotekoshi flood. If the feed cannot be reached, curated reference links are shown. Always cross-check with NDRRMA for casualty figures.
