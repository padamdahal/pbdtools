    About Nepal Standard Time

    Nepal Standard Time (NPT) is UTC+5:45, one of the few time zones in the world that sits at a 45-minute offset. That makes Nepal 15 minutes ahead of India (UTC+5:30) and 5 hours 45 minutes ahead of Coordinated Universal Time. Nepal does not observe daylight saving time, so the offset stays the same all year.

    If you work, study or have family abroad, the difference changes with the season on the other side. The table shows how Nepal compares with places where many Nepalis live and work.

    The Nepali number system

    Nepal groups large numbers in a pattern that differs from the international one. After the first three digits, digits are grouped in pairs: 1,00,000 is one लाख (lakh), 1,00,00,000 is one करोड (crore), and the names continue with अर्ब (arba) and खर्ब (kharba). For example, 1,50,000 is एक लाख पचास हजार.

    Writing amounts in words is common on cheques, contracts, receipts and forms. Use the number to Nepali words converter to do it instantly, including decimals and Nepali digits.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the current time in Nepal?

    The live clock at the top of this page shows the current time in Kathmandu. Nepal uses a single time zone, UTC+5:45, across the whole country.

    Does Nepal use daylight saving time?

    No. Nepal keeps the same time all year, so only the difference with countries that change their clocks varies during the year.

    How do I convert BS to AD?

    Open the BS ↔ AD date converter, pick a date on either side, and the other calendar updates instantly.

    How do I write a number in Nepali words?

    Open the number to Nepali words tool, type the number in English or Nepali digits, and copy the result.

    Does this site collect my data?

    There are no accounts. Your chosen city and 12-hour or 24-hour preference are saved only in your own browser. The weather request is sent with the coordinates of the city you select, not your location.
